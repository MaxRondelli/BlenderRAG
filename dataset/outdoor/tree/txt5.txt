JOSHUA TREE

This script generates a Joshua tree with its distinctive desert appearance, featuring an irregular textured trunk that branches into upward-angled arms, each terminating in a spiky cluster of sharp, cone-shaped leaves that radiate outward. The scene includes a sandy desert ground with scattered rocks and dried leaves at the base, complete with occasional spines at the tips of leaves to capture the harsh, arid environment where these iconic Mojave Desert trees grow.
