BAOBAB TREE

This script generates a baobab tree with its iconic massive bottle-shaped trunk that bulges in the middle and tapers toward both the base and crown, creating the distinctive silhouette of Africa's "upside-down tree." The tree features sparse, gnarled branches spreading horizontally from the top with minimal foliage clustered at the branch ends, accurately representing the baobab's unique adaptation to store water in its enormous trunk during dry seasons.
