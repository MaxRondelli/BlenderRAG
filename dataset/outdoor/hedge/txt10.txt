ROSE HEDGE

This script creates a flowering rose hedge with arching thorny canes bearing dark green leaves and layered roses in pink, red, and white colors distributed throughout the canopy at various heights. Each rose is constructed with multiple layers of overlapping petals (8 petals per layer, 2 layers) arranged around a central sphere to create realistic flower forms, while sharp thorns protrude from the stems at regular intervals, capturing the beauty and defensive nature of a traditional rose hedge.
