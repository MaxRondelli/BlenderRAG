LAVENDER HEDGE

This script generates a low-growing lavender hedge with numerous thin stems topped by purple flower spikes, surrounded by silvery-green elongated foliage clusters at the base that capture the characteristic Mediterranean appearance of lavender plants. The hedge features individual flower spikes rendered as small purple cones rising above the foliage, along with additional spherical purple flower clusters, creating the aromatic and visually striking effect of a lavender border in full bloom.
