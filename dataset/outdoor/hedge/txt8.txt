RED TIP PHOTINIA HEDGE

This script creates a photinia hedge featuring the characteristic red new growth at the tips contrasting with mature dark green foliage below, with burgundy transitional leaves in between to simulate the natural color progression of this popular ornamental species. The layered coloration includes 65% dark green mature leaves forming the base structure, 25% bright red new growth concentrated at the top and branch tips, and 10% burgundy transitional foliage, accurately representing the striking appearance of photinia during active growth periods.
