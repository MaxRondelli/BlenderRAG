WINTER BARE HEDGE

This script generates a dormant winter hedge with bare, gnarled dark branches devoid of leaves but covered with snow accumulation on top surfaces and at branch junctions, set against a snowy ground with drifts at the base. The branches feature irregular twisted growth patterns with thin side twigs extending at various angles, while white snow clusters rest on upper surfaces creating natural accumulation patterns, and larger snow drifts pile up around the hedge base to complete the winter scene.
