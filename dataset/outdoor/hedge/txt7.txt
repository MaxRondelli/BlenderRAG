GOLDEN PRIVET HEDGE

This script generates a golden privet hedge with variegated yellow-green foliage displaying four different shades ranging from bright golden yellow to lime green, creating a luminous and ornamental appearance typical of cultivated golden varieties. The hedge features a semi-trimmed structure with regular branching and side shoots, while the mixed coloration of the leaf clusters provides natural variation and a bright, eye-catching effect suitable for decorative landscape borders.
