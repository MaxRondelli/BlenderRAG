An Islamic minaret 2.5m wide at the base and approximately 20m tall featuring a square decorated base with muqarnas stalactite ornaments, transitioning to a slender cylindrical shaft adorned with five horizontal decorative bands and geometric arabesque patterns. The structure includes a projecting balcony (sharafa) with sixteen ornamental columns, topped by a smaller tapering shaft, an octagonal lantern with arched openings, a green onion dome with muqarnas collar, and crowned with a golden crescent moon (hilal) and star finial, combining light beige stonework with turquoise decorative elements.
