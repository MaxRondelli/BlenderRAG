An Art Deco bell tower 3.0m wide and approximately 18m tall featuring a distinctive stepped setback design with four progressively narrowing tiers decorated with vertical pilaster strips and horizontal decorative bands in brass or gold finish. The structure is crowned with an octagonal bell chamber, ziggurat-style stepped cap, geometric four-sided antenna spire, and sunburst radiating elements, all finished in light beige concrete with metallic gold accents typical of 1920s-30s skyscraper architecture.
