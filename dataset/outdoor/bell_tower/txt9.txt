A Nordic stave church bell tower 2.5m wide and approximately 15m tall constructed from dark wood in three progressively smaller octagonal tiers, each topped with steeply pitched roofs and decorated with stylized dragon head carvings at the roof corners. The tower culminates in a tall pointed spire with a cross, featuring angular cross-braced supports and window openings in the bell chamber, all finished in rich dark brown timber characteristic of Norwegian stavkirke architecture.
