A Chinese pavilion gazebo 4m square and approximately 6m tall featuring four red lacquered columns with ornate golden dougong bracket sets on gray stone bases, connected by dark wood lattice railings with diagonal cross patterns. The structure is crowned with a distinctive double-tiered roof in green glazed tiles with dramatically curved flying eaves that upturn at the corners, decorated with golden dragon ornaments at each corner, topped with a precious pearl finial, and featuring wind chime bells hanging from the eaves.
