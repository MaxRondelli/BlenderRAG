A Mediterranean pergola gazebo 4m long by 3m wide and 2.8m tall featuring four cylindrical white stucco columns with classical bases and capitals supporting weathered wooden beams in a traditional grid pattern with closely-spaced cross-rafters creating dappled shade. The structure is adorned with spiral green vines climbing the columns, four hanging bronze lanterns at the corners, terracotta tile flooring, and decorative terra cotta pots with plants at each outer corner.
