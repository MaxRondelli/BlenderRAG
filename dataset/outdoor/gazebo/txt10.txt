A Gothic Revival gazebo with hexagonal plan 3.5m wide and 6m tall featuring six slender gray stone columns with clustered shafts and ornate capitals adorned with crockets, connected by pointed arches with quatrefoil tracery patterns in wrought iron. The structure is crowned with a tall hexagonal spire decorated with flying buttress ribs and multiple levels of crockets, topped with an ornate cross finial, and includes six pinnacles at each column top, dark oak benches with pointed arch backrests, all executed in authentic medieval Gothic architectural style.
