A modern minimalist gazebo 4m square and 3m tall featuring a polished concrete platform with four slim brushed steel posts supporting a floating cantilevered roof with integrated LED light strips recessed into its underside. The design includes a built-in dark wood bench with backrest along one side, vertical wood slat privacy screen, two geometric planter boxes with angular plants, and delicate steel tension cables connecting post tops to roof corners for structural and aesthetic emphasis.
