The three skyscrapers now feature crystalline faceted geometries instead of smooth curves - the supertall tower has angular diamond-cut sections that spiral upward, the twisted tower incorporates sharp geometric ridges along its rotation, and the parametric tower displays pyramid-stepped terracing at each floor variation. The glass materials shift to deeper, more saturated tones with higher reflectivity - deep sapphire blue, rich amber gold, and emerald green - creating stronger light reflections and more dramatic contrast between the metallic frames.
